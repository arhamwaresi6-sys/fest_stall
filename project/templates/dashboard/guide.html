A BEGINNER'S GUIDE

How to read your overview

A simple guide to sales, costs, profit, and the daily expense schedule.

Start with the date filter

The selected dates control period sales, orders, product costs, scheduled expense deductions, and period net profit. The all-time expense and all-time net profit figures cover the business history, not just those selected dates.

01

Sales revenue

Money from completed orders during the selected dates. Cancelled and refunded orders are not counted as sales.

02

Orders

How many completed orders were made in the selected period.

03

Gross profit

Sales revenue minus the saved product and ingredient costs for those sales. It does not subtract stall rent or other operating expenses. Do not record the same stocked ingredient purchase here as well, or its cost may be counted twice.

Revenue − product costs
04

Expense cost recognized

The scheduled part of expenses charged against profit during the selected dates. It is not necessarily the same as the full amount paid or recorded.

05

All-time position

Shows total expense commitments, how much has been charged to profit, what is still scheduled, and net profit across the full business history.

06

Period net profit

The estimated result for only the selected dates. This can be negative when costs are higher than sales.

Revenue − product costs − scheduled expense cost

A QUICK EXAMPLE

How a 30-day booking is deducted

Suppose a stall booking is 2,500 for 30 days, recorded on November 4.

  1. 1
    November 4The recorded date is the first scheduled day.
    about 83.33 charged
  2. 2
    November 5The second scheduled day is charged.
    about 166.66 charged total
  3. 3
    After the second deductionThe remaining balance is the original cost minus charges through the selected date.
    about 2,333.34 remaining
  4. 4
    By the end of day 30Rounding cents are spread across the schedule.
    2,500.00 charged

The exact daily amount can vary by a cent so all daily deductions add up exactly to the original expense. The expense page shows the original total, charged-to-profit amount, and remaining scheduled balance separately.

WHAT ELSE IS ON THE OVERVIEW

Charts and activity

A

Daily expense deductions

Lists each date in the selected period, the expenses charged on that date, and that day's total. Scroll inside the panel if there are many dates. The period total should match the expense cost used in period net profit.

B

Revenue & expenses chart

Compares sales revenue and scheduled expense cost by day—or by week for longer periods. It uses the same selected date range.

C

Sales revenue trend

Plots completed-order revenue by day—or by week for longer periods—using the selected date range. Hover over a point to see its date and amount.

D

Payment breakdown

Shows recorded cash and bKash payments for completed orders. It tracks how customers paid; it is not another profit calculation.

E

Product performance

Compares products by sales and gross profit before general operating expenses are allocated to products.

F

Recent orders and stock check

Recent orders show latest activity. The stock check highlights products that are low or out of stock.
